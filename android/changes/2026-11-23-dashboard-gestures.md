## 2026-11-23

- Swipe right on a Recents row to preview the deployment and left to open its removal confirmation. Preview and Remove also share a group at the bottom of the row menu.
- Swipe right on Projects to open the workspace sidebar and left to close it. The project ellipsis shows its selected treatment only while its menu is open.
- Swipe between Activity tabs and between Overview and Deployments inside a project. The pages and selection follow the swipe.
- The bottom bar shows icons at rest and briefly reveals a label after a tap. Swipe or hold a sideways drag on Activity to cycle through Recents, Usage, and Alerts.
- Drag sheets between their resting heights or down to dismiss. The page behind a sheet scales back and dims as it opens.
- Row menus animate from their trigger when they open and close.
